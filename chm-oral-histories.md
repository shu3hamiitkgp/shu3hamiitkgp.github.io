## CHM Oral Histories

A curated directory of video interviews from the [Computer History Museum](https://computerhistory.org/oral-histories/) (CHM) YouTube channel — where computing legends share their stories in their own words.

CHM's oral history program has recorded over 1,000 in-depth interviews with the pioneers who built the computing world we live in today. This page highlights some of the most notable ones available on YouTube.

<div class="chm-grid">

  <div class="chm-card">
    <div class="chm-card-header">
      <div class="chm-name"><a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wqI7MrtxPnk" target="_blank">Ken Thompson</a></div>
      <span class="chm-tag">Systems / Unix</span>
    </div>
    <p class="chm-desc">Co-creator of the Unix operating system and the B programming language (precursor to C) at Bell Labs. His work with Dennis Ritchie fundamentally shaped modern operating systems. He also co-designed the Go programming language at Google.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="chm-card">
    <div class="chm-card-header">
      <div class="chm-name"><a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=de2Hsvxaf8M" target="_blank">Dennis Ritchie</a></div>
      <span class="chm-tag">Systems / Languages</span>
    </div>
    <p class="chm-desc">Creator of the C programming language and co-creator of Unix at Bell Labs. His contributions arguably underpin nearly all modern software — C descendants power everything from operating systems to embedded devices. Turing Award winner, 1983 (shared with Thompson).</p>
  </div>

  <div class="chm-card">
    <div class="chm-card-header">
      <div class="chm-name"><a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZO0PXYMVGSU" target="_blank">Bjarne Stroustrup</a></div>
      <span class="chm-tag">Languages</span>
    </div>
    <p class="chm-desc">Designer and original implementer of C++, one of the most widely used programming languages in the world. Created C++ at Bell Labs in the early 1980s, extending C with object-oriented features. His work has deeply influenced software engineering for decades.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="chm-card">
    <div class="chm-card-header">
      <div class="chm-name"><a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bjr7qLeyvlw" target="_blank">Ivan Sutherland</a></div>
      <span class="chm-tag">Graphics / HCI</span>
    </div>
    <p class="chm-desc">Pioneer of computer graphics and human-computer interaction. His 1963 PhD thesis, Sketchpad, was the world's first interactive computer graphics program and laid the foundation for GUIs, CAD, and modern computer graphics. Turing Award winner, 1988.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="chm-card">
    <div class="chm-card-header">
      <div class="chm-name"><a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=29RkHH-psrY" target="_blank">Dave Cutler</a></div>
      <span class="chm-tag">Operating Systems</span>
    </div>
    <p class="chm-desc">Lead architect of Windows NT at Microsoft, which became the foundation for all modern Windows versions. Previously at DEC, he designed the VMS operating system — giving him an extraordinary track record of building reliable, high-performance OS kernels.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="chm-card">
    <div class="chm-card-header">
      <div class="chm-name"><a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Xh8nhK7WS80" target="_blank">Jim Keller</a></div>
      <span class="chm-tag">Hardware / Chips</span>
    </div>
    <p class="chm-desc">Legendary semiconductor architect behind several transformative CPU designs: the AMD K8 (64-bit x86), Apple A4/A5 chips, AMD Zen microarchitecture, and Tesla's Autopilot chip. Known for leading small teams to produce outsized breakthroughs in processor design.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="chm-card">
    <div class="chm-card-header">
      <div class="chm-name"><a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=enHF9lMseP8" target="_blank">Chuck Peddle</a></div>
      <span class="chm-tag">Hardware / Chips</span>
    </div>
    <p class="chm-desc">Lead designer of the MOS Technology 6502 microprocessor, one of the most influential chips in history. The 6502 powered the Apple I, Apple II, Atari 2600, Commodore 64, and Nintendo NES — effectively igniting the personal computer and home gaming revolutions.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="chm-card">
    <div class="chm-card-header">
      <div class="chm-name"><a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=izYWqhUGBGA" target="_blank">Nolan Bushnell</a></div>
      <span class="chm-tag">Gaming / Entrepreneurship</span>
    </div>
    <p class="chm-desc">Founder of Atari and the creator of Pong, the arcade game that launched the video game industry. He also founded Chuck E. Cheese. A serial entrepreneur, Bushnell was an early mentor to Steve Jobs and shaped Silicon Valley's startup culture in the 1970s.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="chm-card">
    <div class="chm-card-header">
      <div class="chm-name"><a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PJxElfc0N9E" target="_blank">Jon Rubinstein</a></div>
      <span class="chm-tag">Hardware / Apple</span>
    </div>
    <p class="chm-desc">Senior VP of Hardware Engineering at Apple and the "father of the iPod," leading the hardware team that created the product. He oversaw the engineering of multiple iconic Apple products including the iMac G3 and PowerBook lines before later becoming CEO of Palm.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="chm-card">
    <div class="chm-card-header">
      <div class="chm-name"><a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CcA4aHKL_bE" target="_blank">John Chambers</a></div>
      <span class="chm-tag">Business / Networking</span>
    </div>
    <p class="chm-desc">CEO of Cisco Systems from 1995 to 2015, growing it from $1.2B to $47B in revenue and transforming it into the backbone of the modern internet. Known for his aggressive acquisition strategy and for navigating Cisco through the dot-com bust.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="chm-card">
    <div class="chm-card-header">
      <div class="chm-name"><a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OmVHkL0IWk4" target="_blank">Ken Thompson (2024 — ACM Turing Award Interview)</a></div>
      <span class="chm-tag">Systems / Unix</span>
    </div>
    <p class="chm-desc">A second, more recent extensive oral history with Ken Thompson conducted in partnership with the ACM, covering his career arc from Unix to C, Plan 9, and Go — with reflections on the evolution of computing over half a century.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="chm-card">
    <div class="chm-card-header">
      <div class="chm-name"><a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wqI7MrtxPnk" target="_blank">Alan Kay</a></div>
      <span class="chm-tag">Programming / OOP / HCI</span>
    </div>
    <p class="chm-desc">Pioneer of object-oriented programming, the Smalltalk language, and the concept of the personal computer (Dynabook). His work at Xerox PARC in the 1970s laid the groundwork for modern GUIs, windowing systems, and the laptop. Turing Award winner, 2003.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="chm-card">
    <div class="chm-card-header">
      <div class="chm-name"><a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7w0HDoAT8dI" target="_blank">Vint Cerf</a></div>
      <span class="chm-tag">Networking / Internet</span>
    </div>
    <p class="chm-desc">Co-designer of the TCP/IP protocols that form the technical foundation of the internet, earning him the title "Father of the Internet." Long-time VP at Google and a tireless advocate for internet access and governance. Turing Award winner, 2004 (shared with Bob Kahn).</p>
  </div>

  <div class="chm-card">
    <div class="chm-card-header">
      <div class="chm-name"><a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2BdBfsXbST8" target="_blank">Donald Knuth</a></div>
      <span class="chm-tag">Algorithms / CS Theory</span>
    </div>
    <p class="chm-desc">Author of the multi-volume magnum opus <em>The Art of Computer Programming</em>, which Bill Gates called essential reading for any serious programmer. Creator of the TeX typesetting system. His work on algorithm analysis and data structures shaped the entire discipline of computer science. Turing Award winner, 1974.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="chm-card">
    <div class="chm-card-header">
      <div class="chm-name"><a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=A4oLRnG2q3Q" target="_blank">Linus Torvalds</a></div>
      <span class="chm-tag">Operating Systems / Open Source</span>
    </div>
    <p class="chm-desc">Creator of the Linux kernel, which powers the vast majority of the world's servers, Android phones, and supercomputers. Also created Git, the distributed version control system now used by virtually every software project worldwide. One of the most impactful open-source contributors in history.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="chm-card">
    <div class="chm-card-header">
      <div class="chm-name"><a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0-EZMvLDrX4" target="_blank">Steve Wozniak</a></div>
      <span class="chm-tag">Hardware / Apple</span>
    </div>
    <p class="chm-desc">Co-founder of Apple Computer and sole designer of the Apple I and Apple II — the machines that launched the personal computer industry. Widely regarded as one of the greatest hardware engineers of all time, he combined deep engineering brilliance with a hacker's ingenuity.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="chm-card">
    <div class="chm-card-header">
      <div class="chm-name"><a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bQjxqJFHwBQ" target="_blank">Gordon Moore</a></div>
      <span class="chm-tag">Semiconductors / Intel</span>
    </div>
    <p class="chm-desc">Co-founder of Intel and author of Moore's Law — the 1965 observation that transistor density on chips doubles roughly every two years. This "law" became a self-fulfilling prophecy that guided the semiconductor industry for decades, underpinning the exponential progress of computing.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="chm-card">
    <div class="chm-card-header">
      <div class="chm-name"><a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=izYWqhUGBGA" target="_blank">Jensen Huang</a></div>
      <span class="chm-tag">Hardware / AI / GPUs</span>
    </div>
    <p class="chm-desc">Co-founder and CEO of NVIDIA, the company that invented the GPU and became the dominant force in AI compute. Under his three-decade leadership, NVIDIA transformed from a gaming chip company into the engine of the modern AI revolution, with a market cap surpassing $3 trillion.</p>
  </div>
